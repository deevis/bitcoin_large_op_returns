GATEKEEP — CONCEPT DOCUMENT v1.0
Date: 2026-05-02

CORE INSIGHT
------------
The conditions under which a person grants access to themselves
are a form of self-expression — as meaningful as a profile photo
or a bio. A chosen challenge is a behavioral profile, not a
declarative one. Solving it is a signal of intent, patience,
and minimal effort investment.

CONCEPT
-------
A mobile application (working title: Gatekeep) where each user
configures a challenge that must be completed before another
person can initiate contact with them — socially or romantically.

The challenge is chosen freely by the user and can range from:
- Trivial (tap a button 3 times)
- Casual (reach score 512 in 2048)
- Demanding (reach score 2048 in 2048)
- Custom (logic puzzle, mini-game, reaction test, etc.)

Only successful completion of the challenge unlocks the contact
information or opens a communication channel with the target user.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
-----------------
1. Asymmetry replaced by friction.
   Unlike existing platforms where sending a message costs zero
   effort, Gatekeep introduces a minimal but real cost: time and
   attention. This reduces spam and low-intent contact.

2. The challenge reveals the challenger.
   What someone chooses as their gate says something about who
   they want to meet. A high threshold selects for patience and
   persistence. A playful challenge selects for a playful person.

3. The attempt reveals the initiator.
   Someone who completes a 3-minute game to reach you has
   demonstrated more intent than someone who typed "hey".

4. Symmetry as ideal.
   The most elegant implementation requires both parties to
   complete each other's challenge before communication opens.
   This transforms the gate from a test into a shared ritual.

5. Opt-in by design.
   No universal enforcement is claimed or intended. This is a
   voluntary social contract among users who share its values.

PRIMARY USE CASES
-----------------
A. Social networking — protection against low-effort, high-volume
   contact. Particularly relevant for people with high inbound
   social demand.

B. Dating — replacing the swipe (zero-cost signal) with a
   behavioral challenge (minimal-cost signal). Unlike paywalls
   (which filter for wealth), this filters for engagement and
   patience. Target positioning: adjacent to Hinge ("designed
   to be deleted"), not Tinder.

C. Gated communities — shared challenge as membership ritual.
   Everyone in the group has passed the same gate, creating
   implicit common ground before first words are exchanged.

DIFFERENTIATOR VS EXISTING PLATFORMS
--------------------------------------
Existing friction mechanisms on dating/social apps are financial
(subscriptions, boosts, super likes). They select for disposable
income. Gatekeep's friction is behavioral. It selects for
intentionality, patience, and playfulness — qualities more
predictive of interaction quality than purchasing power.

PROTOTYPE SCOPE (v0.1)
-----------------------
Stack: React Native + Expo, Supabase
Single challenge type: 2048 (configurable threshold: 512/1024/2048)
Flow: Register -> Configure threshold -> Generate QR code ->
      Challenger scans QR -> Plays 2048 -> On success: contact revealed
No in-app chat in v0.1. Contact info revealed as plain text.
Deep link scheme: gatekeep://challenge/{userId}

MONETIZATION HYPOTHESIS (unexplored)
--------------------------------------
- Freemium: basic challenges free, premium challenge types paid
- B2B: white-label for communities, events, professional networks
- Licensing to existing dating platforms
