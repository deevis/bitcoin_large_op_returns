enigma   # LLMs are the blockchain of 2026
Lets rewind a few years back in 2018-2022. Blockchain technology was revolutionary and everyone tried to put in into various things - real estate,  banking system,  even facebook tried LIBRA.Ultimately, nothing worked! Blockchain has a niche and effective usecase which is of *decentralized payments*. Now I see LLMs panning out the same way, stuffing LLMs into everything is not working out. It has few very good use cases like search, text summarization and boiler plate coding.  
> Lets not try to put tomato sauce in everything. I love my dosa crisp and buttery, please don't ruin it!
